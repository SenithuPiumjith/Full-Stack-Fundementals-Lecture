Welcome to Travel Sri Lanka

Sri Lanka, often called the Pearl of the Indian Ocean, is a vibrant tropical island packed with ancient temples, misty tea plantations, and golden beaches. Visitors can explore the historic Cultural Triangle, spot elephants and leopards on safari in Yala National Park, or ride the scenic hill country trains through Ella. With a warm climate and rich 3,000-year-old heritage, it offers an affordable and diverse island getaway.

Destinations

Kandy

Kandy, the vibrant cultural capital of Sri Lanka, is nestled in the island's lush central highlands. As the last independent capital of the ancient Sinhalese monarchy, this UNESCO World Heritage Site is deeply revered as the home of the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic (Sri Dalada Maligawa), which houses a sacred tooth of the Buddha. The city centers around the scenic, man-made Kandy Lake, offering a peaceful escape from the bustling streets filled with historic architecture, vibrant markets, and traditional Kandyan dance performances. Surrounded by mist-covered mountain ranges and sprawling tea plantations, its cooler highland climate makes it an essential stop for anyone exploring the country's rich spiritual and royal heritage.
